Privacy Policy

BgBgGames · Effective date: 25 October 2026

In short: our games collect a random game/device ID, gameplay and technical events, and — if you allow it — data used for personalized ads. We use them to run the games, fix problems, understand which levels are too hard, show leaderboards and show ads. Some games record gameplay logs (your in-game actions) so that we can replay a session and see where players get stuck. Microphone sound and camera images are never recorded or sent to us. We do not sell your data for money. You can ask us to delete your data at any time: bgbgvladimir@gmail.com.

2. What data we collect

2.1 Data collected automatically

  • Identifiers: a random ID created by the Game on first launch; IDs created by our analytics and ad partners (for example the Firebase installation ID, the AppMetrica device ID); the Android Advertising ID / App Set ID (only where you allow it — see section 5).
  • Device and app information: device model and manufacturer, operating system and version, language and region settings, screen size, app version, and which hardware features and sensors the device has (to find out on which devices a puzzle does not work).
  • Gameplay and usage data: sessions, levels started and completed, time spent, hints used, settings, in-game events.
  • Diagnostics: crash reports, performance data and technical logs.
  • Gameplay logs and session replays: in some Games we record the sequence of your in-game actions — taps, swipes and button presses with their screen positions and timing, game state changes and, where a puzzle depends on them, values of motion and light sensors. We use these recordings to replay a play session, find levels that are too hard or confusing, and reproduce bugs. Recordings are linked only to the random game ID; they do not contain microphone sound, camera images, screenshots of other apps, or text you type outside the Game.
  • Network data: IP address, used by us and our partners to deliver the service, prevent fraud and estimate your approximate location (country/region). We do not collect precise (GPS) location.
  • Advertising data: which ads were shown and how you interacted with them.

2.2 Data you provide

  • Nickname for leaderboards and ratings (optional). It is visible to other players — do not use your real name.
  • Chat messages in Games that have a chat. They are visible to other players.
  • Support messages you send us by email, including your email address.

2.3 Device permissions and sensors

Some Games use the microphone, motion sensors (accelerometer, gyroscope), the light sensor or other device features as part of the gameplay. Microphone sound and camera images are processed only on your device, in real time, and are never recorded, stored or sent to us or to anyone else. From motion and light sensors we may send only simple values (for example “the phone was shaken”, tilt or brightness level) as part of the gameplay logs described above. You can refuse or withdraw a permission in your device settings; the related puzzles may then be skipped.

Our Games do not access your contacts, photos, files, SMS or precise location.

5. Advertising and your choices

  • Free Games show ads from the networks above. If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, we ask for your consent before personalized ads are shown (consent window based on Google’s consent framework). Without consent you see non-personalized ads only.
  • You can change your choice at any time in the Game (Settings → Privacy, where available) or by contacting us.
  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads) → Delete advertising ID stops apps from using it for personalized ads.
  • Rewarded ads are always optional.

6. International transfers

Some providers process data outside the European Economic Area (for example in the United States). Where this happens, the transfer is protected by the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or another safeguard recognised by the GDPR. You can ask us for details.

7. How long we keep data

  • Gameplay analytics: as long as needed to improve the Games, then deleted or anonymized (usually within 2 years).
  • Gameplay logs and session replays: up to 12 months.
  • Leaderboard entries and nicknames: while the leaderboard exists or until you ask us to delete them.
  • Support emails: up to 2 years after the issue is resolved.
  • Data we must keep by law: for the period the law requires.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to: access your data; correct it; have it deleted; restrict or object to its processing (including processing based on legitimate interest); receive it in a portable format; and withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier processing.

To use any of these rights, email bgbgvladimir@gmail.com with the name of the Game and, if possible, your in-game nickname or ID. We answer within 30 days.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority — in Poland the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO), or the authority in your country.

9. Additional information for US residents

Depending on your state (e.g. California), you have the right to know what personal information we collect, to request its deletion or correction, and to opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information for targeted advertising. To opt out, delete or reset your device advertising ID as described in section 5, or email us. We will not discriminate against you for using these rights.

10. Children

Our Games are not directed at children under 13, and in the EEA not at children under the age at which they can consent to data processing themselves (13–16 depending on the country; 16 in Poland). We do not knowingly collect personal data from such children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Our servers and databases are access-restricted and protected with passwords and keys known only to us. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your data.
